About Integrated Pest Management

The Scientific Approach to Pest Control

Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is a science-based process that identifies and reduces risks from pests through inspection, identification, treatment, and prevention.

We don't just eliminate pests—we implement preventive measures to ensure they don't return, using environmentally responsible methods.
